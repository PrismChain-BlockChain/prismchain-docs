# 🚀 PrismChain Community

> **Build the ecosystem together. Share what you discover.**

The PrismChain community is where people gather around the ecosystem, exchange ideas, share work, ask questions, and discover what can be built with it.

GitHub provides the permanent public record.

Discord provides the living conversation.

They serve different purposes.

```text
GITHUB
Permanent knowledge
Documentation
Research
Evidence
Architecture
History
        ↕
DISCORD
Discussion
Questions
Ideas
Collaboration
Community
Real-time conversation
```

The community is not separate from the technical work.

It is the environment where people discover it, question it, contribute to it, and build around it.

---

# 1. Community Principles

The PrismChain community follows a few simple principles.

### Build openly

Share what can responsibly be shared.

### Ask questions

Questions improve understanding.

### Challenge ideas

A strong idea should survive scrutiny.

### Show your work

Claims are stronger when they can be examined.

### Respect uncertainty

Not knowing is better than pretending to know.

### Give credit

Research, ideas, code, artwork, documentation, and contributions should be attributed appropriately.

### Protect what should remain private

Open source does not require the publication of every internal mechanism.

---

# 2. Community Status Language

The same distinction used throughout the technical documentation applies to community discussion:

🟢 **Built / Demonstrated**

🔵 **Research**

🟣 **Experimental**

🟡 **Hypothesis / Planned**

⚪ **Historical**

🔴 **Private**

Community discussion should not turn:

> “We are considering this”

into:

> “This exists.”

Likewise, an experiment should not automatically become a promise.

---

# 3. 💬 General

The general community space is for broad PrismChain ecosystem discussion.

Topics may include:

* PrismChain
* Rainbow Ring
* Spectral Dyad
* FractaChain
* ecosystem developments
* community questions
* interesting discoveries
* public research
* public announcements
* general conversation

Keep technical claims appropriately scoped.

If a discussion becomes architectural, research-oriented, or implementation-specific, move it into the appropriate technical or research area.

# 5. 💡 Ideas

Ideas are welcome.

They should simply be identified as ideas.

A useful idea can become:

```text
IDEA
  ↓
QUESTION
  ↓
HYPOTHESIS
  ↓
RESEARCH
  ↓
EXPERIMENT
  ↓
EVIDENCE
  ↓
ARCHITECTURAL DECISION
```

An idea does not automatically become a feature.

It does not automatically become part of PrismChain.

It does not automatically become part of the roadmap.

The community should be free to explore ideas without confusing exploration with commitment.

> **Ideas are invitations to investigate.**

---

Showcase is for community work built around the public ecosystem.

Examples may include:

* applications;
* visualizations;
* educational material;
* integrations;
* experiments;
* artwork;
* tools;
* research;
* demonstrations;
* community projects;
* documentation;

**What was built**

from

**What is planned**

and from

**What is experimental.**

A community project does not automatically become an official PrismChain component.

---

# 7. 🧃 Off-Topic

Not every conversation needs to be about blockchain, mathematics, or architecture.

Off-topic discussion provides room for the community to interact as people.

The same basic expectations still apply:

* respect others;
* avoid harassment;
* avoid spam;
* respect community rules;
* do not misrepresent personal opinions as official PrismChain positions.

The ecosystem can be technically serious without making every conversation technical.

---

# 10. Public vs Official

Community discussion should not automatically be interpreted as an official PrismChain position.

There is an important distinction between:

```text
COMMUNITY IDEA
      ≠
RESEARCH RESULT
      ≠
DOCUMENTED ARCHITECTURE
      ≠
OFFICIAL IMPLEMENTATION
```

A community member may propose an idea that is never adopted.

That is healthy.

The ecosystem should have room for exploration without requiring every idea to become official.

---

# 11. Community and GitHub

GitHub is where durable public artifacts should live.

Examples:

```text
DISCUSSION
    ↓
IDEA
    ↓
RESEARCH
    ↓
DOCUMENTATION
    ↓
EVIDENCE
    ↓
PERMANENT ARTIFACT
```

Discord conversations can inspire documentation.

Research can produce papers.

Experiments can produce evidence.

Community projects can become showcased artifacts.

But the permanent record should be updated deliberately.

---

# 12. Community and Research

The community can participate in discovery.

A community question may reveal:

* an overlooked assumption;
* a useful experiment;
* a mathematical relationship;
* a security concern;
* an architectural weakness;
* or a new direction.

The research process remains evidence-driven.

Community enthusiasm is not evidence.

Community skepticism is not proof of failure.

Both can be valuable inputs.

---

# 13. Community and Architecture

Architecture should not change simply because an idea becomes popular.

Likewise, an unpopular technical truth should not be ignored.

Architectural decisions should follow:

```text
QUESTION
   ↓
RESEARCH
   ↓
EXPERIMENT
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
REVIEW
   ↓
DECISION
```

The community can influence the questions.

Evidence determines what survives.

---

# 14. Community Culture

The goal is a community that is:

**Curious without being careless.**

**Ambitious without pretending.**

**Technical without being exclusionary.**

**Open without exposing what should remain private.**

**Experimental without confusing experiments with facts.**

**Creative without confusing creativity with specification.**

---

# 15. What the Community Should Protect

The community should protect:

* intellectual honesty;
* attribution;
* constructive criticism;
* useful disagreement;
* research integrity;
* security awareness;
* privacy;
* intellectual property;
* and the distinction between fact and speculation.

A healthy technical ecosystem should be able to say:

> “We don't know yet.”

That is not weakness.

It is an accurate status.

---

# 17. Community Rule of Thumb

When discussing an idea, ask:

**Is this built?**

**Is this research?**

**Is this experimental?**

**Is this hypothetical?**

**Is this historical?**

**Is this private?**

That simple distinction prevents a large amount of confusion.
