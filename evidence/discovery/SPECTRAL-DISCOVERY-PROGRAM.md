# 1. Purpose

The original PrismChain evidence suite demonstrates the architecture of PrismChain itself.

This suite asks a deeper question:

> **Can the mathematical framework derived from the spectral structure of light become a useful general-purpose system for mathematical investigation, discovery, and creation?**

The purpose is **not** to assume that Spectral Mathematics works.

The purpose is to test it.

The experiments therefore progress from:

```text

  ↓
```

Every stage must produce evidence.

# 2. Foundational Hypothesis

Spectral Mathematics proposes that the structure of light can be represented as a mathematical coordinate system.

The proposed **Light Calculator** is the conceptual name for the natural spectral grid produced by the relationships among properties such as:

* wavelength
* phase
* amplitude
* angular relationships
* frequency
* interference
* resonance
* propagation
* transformation

The hypothesis is that mathematical objects can be represented within this spectral space and that operations performed within that space may reveal relationships that are difficult to observe in conventional representations.

This is a hypothesis.

The experiments must determine whether it is useful, reproducible, and mathematically meaningful.
