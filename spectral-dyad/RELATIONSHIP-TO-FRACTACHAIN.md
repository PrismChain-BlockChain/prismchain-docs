# 🧠 Spectral Dyad — Relationship to FractaChain

> **Spectral Dyad explores intelligence and guidance. FractaChain explores recursive and fractal structure.**

Spectral Dyad and FractaChain are complementary parts of the broader ecosystem, but they are distinct architectural domains.

Spectral Dyad is concerned with intelligence, observation, interpretation, intent, and guidance.

FractaChain is concerned with fractal and recursive structures, memory, representation, and the mathematical relationships that may emerge from those structures.

Their relationship is therefore primarily a **research and architectural relationship**, not a claim that one system is simply a component of the other.

# 13. The Mathematical Research Question

A deeper research question connects the projects:

> **Is there a natural mathematical relationship between spectral structure and fractal structure that can be expressed computationally?**

This is more precise than assuming that the relationship already exists.

The research process should investigate:

* whether the structures are mathematically compatible;
* whether meaningful mappings exist;
* whether those mappings preserve useful information;
* whether they provide computational advantages;
* whether they support memory or representation;
* whether they can be expressed without arbitrary translation layers;
* whether the relationship remains useful when implemented.

The objective is to **discover the relationship**, not invent one merely because the architectures appear complementary.

# 27. Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Keep the projects distinct

Spectral Dyad and FractaChain are separate architectural domains.

### Principle 2 — Research the relationship

Do not assume the relationship before the mathematics and experiments establish it.

### Principle 3 — Follow the mathematics

Let mathematical structure inform architecture rather than forcing mathematics to justify predetermined architecture.

### Principle 4 — Preserve independence

Neither project should require the other unless evidence demonstrates a meaningful dependency.

### Principle 5 — Treat recursive structure as a research opportunity

Fractal and recursive mathematics may provide useful representations for memory, context, and intelligence.

### Principle 6 — Preserve uncertainty

A proposed relationship remains a hypothesis until supported by evidence.

### Principle 7 — Protect proprietary mechanisms

Public architecture does not require disclosure of unpublished mathematics or implementation.

### Principle 8 — Evidence determines capability

A conceptual relationship becomes an architectural claim only when it is demonstrated.
