# 🧠 Spectral Dyad — Status

> **Status describes what is known. It does not describe what we hope is true.**

Spectral Dyad is an evolving intelligence-oriented architecture within the PrismChain ecosystem.

Its public role is centered on:

* observation;
* context;
* interpretation;
* intent;
* guidance;
* relationship awareness;
* and research into intelligent interaction with the broader ecosystem.

The architecture continues to evolve through research, experimentation, implementation, and evidence.

This document exists to distinguish what is currently established from what remains under research, experimental development, planned work, or private investigation.

# 4. What Is Established

The following architectural statements are part of the current public position.

### Spectral Dyad is an intelligence layer

Spectral Dyad occupies an intelligence-oriented role within the PrismChain ecosystem.

### PrismChain remains the computational blockchain

PrismChain is the seven-layer blockchain.

```text id="c3x8m1"
RED
ORANGE
YELLOW
GREEN
BLUE
INDIGO
VIOLET
```

These seven layers produce the unified computational result represented by the White Light Block.

### Spectral Dyad does not add an eighth PrismChain layer

The Dyad exists in relationship to PrismChain.

It is not an additional spectral layer.

### Rainbow Ring remains the relationship layer

Spectral Dyad does not replace Rainbow Ring.

### FractaChain remains a separate architecture

Spectral Dyad and FractaChain are related through research but are not presented as the same system.

# 24. Evidence Requirements

A public capability claim should ideally answer:

```text id="x8p4nz"
WHAT WAS CLAIMED?

WHAT WAS TESTED?

WHAT WAS OBSERVED?

WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT?

WHAT ARE THE LIMITATIONS?

WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN?
```

This standard applies especially to intelligence-related claims.

The more extraordinary the claim, the stronger the evidence required.

# 25. What Would Change Status?

A research concept can move toward a demonstrated capability when evidence supports the transition.

For example:

```text id="u4n7qb"
HYPOTHESIS
     ↓
EXPERIMENT
     ↓
REPEATABLE RESULT
     ↓
DOCUMENTED EVIDENCE
     ↓
DEMONSTRATED CAPABILITY
```

Likewise, an existing assumption can move backward if evidence contradicts it.

Status is therefore not a permanent declaration of success.

It is a record of current understanding.

# 28. The Status Principle

The most important status rule is:

> **The documentation should describe the state of knowledge, not the desired state of the project.**

If something works, demonstrate it.

If something is being researched, label it research.

If something is only a hypothesis, say so.

If something is private, protect it.

If something fails, document the lesson when appropriate.

This allows the public record to evolve without rewriting history or overstating capability.
