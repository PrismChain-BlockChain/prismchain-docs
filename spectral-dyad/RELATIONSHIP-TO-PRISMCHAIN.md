# 🧠 Spectral Dyad — Relationship to PrismChain

> **Spectral Dyad guides. PrismChain computes.**

Spectral Dyad and PrismChain are related parts of the same ecosystem, but they are not the same system and they do not perform the same function.

The distinction is fundamental:

**Spectral Dyad is the intelligence layer.**

**PrismChain is the seven-layer blockchain.**

Spectral Dyad can observe, interpret, identify intent, and provide guidance.

PrismChain performs its own seven-layer computation and produces the White Light Block.

Neither system replaces the other.
