# 🔬 PrismChain Research

> **Research comes before certainty. Evidence comes before claims.**

The PrismChain ecosystem is built from an ongoing program of mathematical, architectural, computational, and systems research.

This repository documents that research publicly where appropriate.

The purpose is not to make every idea appear finished.

The purpose is to preserve the path from **question → investigation → evidence → understanding**.

---

PrismChain research investigates the structures, relationships, and mathematical ideas that can support the PrismChain ecosystem.

* fractal relationships;
* intelligence;

* evidence and verification.

Some research produces established architecture.

Some produces useful negative results.

Some remains unresolved.

Some may eventually be discarded.

That is part of research.

# 5. Research Status Language

The public research library uses the following status system:

| Status                      | Meaning                                              |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| 🟢 **Built / Demonstrated** | Supported by implementation or demonstrated evidence |
| 🔵 **Research**             | Actively being investigated                          |
| 🟣 **Experimental**         | Being tested through controlled experimentation      |
| 🟡 **Hypothesis / Planned** | Proposed but not sufficiently established            |
| 🔴 **Private**              | Intentionally undisclosed                            |

These labels prevent research ideas from being mistaken for finished capabilities.

A concept can move between categories as evidence changes.

---

# 6. Research Is Not a Specification

Research documentation is not automatically an implementation specification.

A mathematical hypothesis may be investigated without becoming architecture.

An architectural idea may be tested and rejected.

An experiment may reveal that an assumption was incorrect.

A research paper may describe an earlier stage of thinking that no longer represents the current architecture.

Therefore:

> **Research documents what is being learned. Specifications describe what has been decided.**

The two should remain distinguishable.

# 8. Mathematics Before Implementation

The research program favors understanding the underlying structure before forcing it into software.

The guiding sequence is:

```text
UNDERSTAND
    ↓
FORMALIZE
    ↓
MODEL
    ↓
TEST
    ↓
IMPLEMENT
```

This does not mean implementation must wait for perfect mathematics.

Implementation and experimentation can reveal information that changes the model.

The relationship is therefore iterative:

```text
MATHEMATICS
     ↕
MODEL
     ↕
EXPERIMENT
     ↕
IMPLEMENTATION
     ↕
EVIDENCE
```

The important principle is that evidence is allowed to change the architecture.

A failed experiment should be documented when useful.

An unresolved question should remain unresolved.

# 18. Public and Private Research

Not all research should be published.

The public research library may intentionally exclude:

* unpublished mathematics;
* proprietary algorithms;
* private prompts;
* internal AI orchestration;
* confidential datasets;
* proprietary experiments;
* security-sensitive mechanisms;
* competitive implementation details.

Public documentation should reveal enough to establish the architecture and research direction without surrendering the mechanisms that provide competitive advantage.

The governing principle is:

> **Reveal the architecture. Protect the advantage.**

# 20. Research Culture

The research culture is:

### Ask questions.

Do not assume the answer.

### Follow the mathematics.

Do not force the mathematics to support the desired architecture.

### Test assumptions.

Do not treat intuition as evidence.

### Record failures.

A failed experiment can eliminate an incorrect path.

### Preserve uncertainty.

Unknown is a legitimate research state.

### Let evidence change the architecture.

The architecture is not sacred when evidence demonstrates a better model.

### Protect legitimate intellectual property.

Public transparency does not require publishing every mechanism.

The central research principle is:
