# 📐 PrismChain Research — Mathematics

> **Mathematics is where the architecture must become precise.**

The Mathematics section documents the public mathematical foundations, structures, relationships, and open questions underlying the PrismChain ecosystem.

Its purpose is not to claim that every architectural concept has already been mathematically proven.

Its purpose is to establish a disciplined boundary between:

* established mathematics;
* mathematical interpretation;
* computational abstraction;
* research hypotheses;
* experimental models;
* and unpublished mathematics.

The guiding principle is:

> **If a relationship matters, define it mathematically.**

---

# 2. Established Mathematics vs. New Research

The public research program distinguishes established mathematics from new mathematical work.

### Established mathematics

Mathematical results already established through the broader mathematical literature.

### Interpretation

Using established mathematics to describe a new architectural problem.

### Hypothesis

A proposed mathematical relationship that has not yet been sufficiently established.

### New mathematical research

A potentially novel relationship, structure, transformation, or formalism being investigated.

### Computational model

A representation of a mathematical relationship in software or another formal computational system.

These categories must not be conflated.

> **Using established mathematics does not automatically prove a new architectural claim.**

# 27. Mathematical Evidence

Mathematical claims require appropriate forms of evidence.

Depending on the claim, evidence may include:

* derivation;
* proof;
* formal definitions;
* counterexamples;
* numerical analysis;
* symbolic analysis;
* simulation;
* reproducible computation;
* independent verification;
* experimental results.

A simulation can provide evidence about a model.

It is not automatically a mathematical proof.

Likewise, an implementation that produces an expected output does not automatically prove the underlying theory.

# 31. Mathematics Before Claims

A central discipline of the PrismChain research program is:

> **Do not use mathematical language to make an idea sound more advanced than it is.**

If a term has a precise mathematical meaning, use that meaning.

If a relationship is only conceptual, identify it as conceptual.

If a relationship is hypothetical, identify it as hypothetical.

If a relationship has been demonstrated, provide evidence.

This protects both the research and the credibility of the public record.
